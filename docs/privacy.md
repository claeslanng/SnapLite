# SnapLite privacy policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

SnapLite is a screenshot tool: a Windows app and a browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave.
In short: **SnapLite does not collect, send or sell any data. Your screenshots stay on your computer.**

## The browser extension

- **What it accesses:** only the tab you're looking at, and only when you click the SnapLite button
  or press one of its shortcuts (the `activeTab` permission). It can't see your other tabs or your
  browsing history, and it doesn't run in the background on the pages you visit.
- **What it does with that tab:** it takes screenshots while scrolling, temporarily hides cookie
  banners and floating buttons, and measures where the page's sections start. Then it puts the page
  back exactly as it was.
- **Where the screenshots go:** to the SnapLite app on the same computer, through the browser's
  native messaging. If the app isn't installed, they go to your Downloads folder. Nothing is sent
  to any server, not to us and not to anyone else.
- **What it stores:** your choices in the popup (for example "hide cookie banners"), in the
  browser's own extension storage on your computer.
- **No tracking:** no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, no remote code.

## The Windows app

- It saves screenshots to the folder you choose (by default `Pictures\SnapLite`) and can copy them
  to the clipboard.
- Its settings are stored in `%APPDATA%\SnapLite`. Logs, kept only when something goes wrong, are
  stored in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SnapLite`.
- The "Full webpage" capture opens the web address you type in a hidden browser on your computer.
  That page is loaded from the website like any normal visit; SnapLite itself sends nothing anywhere.
- It doesn't check for updates or contact any server on its own.
